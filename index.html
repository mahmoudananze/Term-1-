Grammar

Six exam structures with practice

Open each lesson to see the rule and an example.

Zero conditional

If + present simple, present simple.

If it rains, the soil absorbs water.
Comparatives

cooler than · more efficient than · better than

A shaded roof is cooler than an exposed roof.
Present simple passive

am / is / are + past participle

Rainwater is stored in a tank.
Indirect questions

Use statement word order after the question word.

Could you tell me where the water goes?
Linkers

however (contrast) · moreover (addition) · therefore (result)

It is costly. However, it offers long-term savings.
Sequencing

First · Next · Then · Finally

First, inspect the roof. Next, add the protective layer.

Reading

Two texts · 16 questions · 40 marks

Use the same five steps for every question.

  1. Skim
    Read the title and first lines to get the topic.
  2. Read the question
    Underline the key words.
  3. Locate evidence
    Find the sentence that answers it.
  4. Eliminate unsupported options
    Cross out options the text does not support.
  5. Check the complete answer
    Make sure every part of your answer is true.
Specific information

Find the exact name, number, example or condition.

Inference

Combine textual clues, not personal opinions.

Overall meaning

Choose the answer that covers the entire text.

Tone

Watch for careful words like may, depends and tends to.

Essay plan

  1. Introduction
    Introduce both options and your opinion.
  2. Body paragraph 1
    Compare one feature of both options, with an example.
  3. Body paragraph 2
    Compare another feature and explain the effect.
  4. Body paragraph 3
    Justify your preference with a realistic example.
  5. Conclusion
    Summarise and confirm your judgement.
